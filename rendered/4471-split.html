The queued parser fix closes parser only. hint and shift stay open after it ships.

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant’s date comes out with day and month swapped: “4 March turned into 3 April”. Fixed by the queued parser change.
  • hint: booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it day first. Same symptom, different code: the fix is the hint (or the form’s reading), not the parser.
  • shift: the date moves by one day, not by a swap. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” A swap cannot do that; this looks like a time zone conversion. The desk already listed these reports in desk-notes.json as a one-day move.

How the reports were counted

  • One id, one report: the export pages overlap, so repeated rows count once (uniqueById).
  • Cause by tenant and channel (causes.ts), not by intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the guess disagrees with the tenant often (compare “tagged as” below).
  • A report the desk read as a one-day move is shift, whatever its tenant.
